## Using this queue

Target an entry with `$hive-security security-audit v5.0.0-Vx.y.z`. The audit must inspect all relevant paths and controls for that requirement, record findings or an explicit no-issue/blocked/not-assessed disposition, and continue through every requested ID. The default audit checks the complete applicable L1+L2 scope. Update the latest-audit column after each targeted or full audit; dated reports remain the record of the complete review.

Estimated priorities are provisional risk triage for ASVS gaps. The priority basis is recorded per entry and follows the rubric in the skill reference; confirm estimates with Hive-specific evidence during audit. Needs-information entries have no severity estimate until the missing evidence is supplied. CVSS is reserved for confirmed vulnerabilities and uses FIRST’s [current CVSS specification](https://www.first.org/cvss/specification-document).
